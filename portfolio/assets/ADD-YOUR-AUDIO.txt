ADD YOUR ELEVENLABS RECORDING HERE

1. Rename the exported MP3 to introduction.mp3 (not introduction.mp3.mp3).
2. Place it inside this assets folder, beside this instruction file.
3. Open index.html with assets beside it, or serve this folder and refresh.
   The player already looks for assets/introduction.mp3. No source edits required.

The recording attempts playback AFTER the welcome greeting closes.
If the browser blocks autoplay, press Play in the bottom-right “Meet Ayush” player.
Stop ends playback and rewinds to the beginning. Your off preference is saved
where browser storage is available. Audio never loops automatically.

OPTIONAL — make the HTML fully portable WITH your recording:
Run: python scripts/bundle.py
The MP3 will be embedded into index.html and standalone.html, so you can then
share either HTML without a separate assets folder. This increases HTML size.

No recording, demo tone, fake voice or ElevenLabs credentials are included.
Use a recording you have permission to publish.
